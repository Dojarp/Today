# 💜 Today - NotYourTodoAPP

> **Today — because getting things done shouldn't feel like making a to-do list.**

A simple little task app for when you have a bunch of things floating around in your head and just want to get them down.

Instead of building a perfect to-do list, just tell **Today** what's on your mind.

It takes the messy thought and turns it into a clean list of things you can actually do.

## ☁️ Why Today?

Most to-do apps make you *manage tasks*.

**Today** is more about getting them out of your head.

You don't have to sit there thinking:

`"How do I organize this?"`

Just type or talk:

`"umm I i need to go to the gym andd aa umm maybeee finish my assignment and call mom"`

And let **Today** handle the rest.

## 💜 What makes it different?

🎙️ **Talk instead of type**  
Sometimes it's just easier to say what's on your mind. Voice input lets you do exactly that.

✦ **Messy input is okay**  
You don't need to phrase things perfectly. Say it how you would normally say it.

◌ **One thought → multiple tasks**  
Give it a bunch of things at once and it breaks them down into individual tasks.

✓ **Just focus on today**  
No complicated productivity systems. No endless dashboards. No unnecessary stuff.

## 💜 The idea

Today isn't trying to be another giant productivity platform.

It's just a small place to answer one simple question:

**What do I need to get done today?**
